Design and Fabrication of an FSAE Car Undertray

Designed and fabricated an undertray for the Binghamton Motorsports FSAE team within rulebook constraints. Fusion 360 and ANSYS were used for design and validation. The carbon fibre component was fabricated through wet layup and vacuum bagging, then tested via tufting.

Design, Analysis and Optimisation of a Race Car Rear Wing

Leveraged CAD and CFD tools to design a range of rear wing configurations in compliance with the FIA 2026 Formula 1 regulations. Key design considerations included the trade-off between downforce and drag, and overall aerodynamic efficiency.

03
Vehicle Dynamics Optimisation of a Half Car Model

Used the Runge-Kutta method to simulate suspension response when encountering an obstacle, then applied Bayesian Optimisation to tune spring stiffness, damping coefficient and centre of gravity position. A plug-and-play model allowing user-defined inputs for mass, wheelbase, velocity and obstacle geometry.
